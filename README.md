# fly-in

A fly goes to the cinema. A director turns a video (or audio plus images) into a scene, a real
fruit-fly brain (the FlyWire connectome) lives through it, and an orchestra turns its recorded
brain activity into a soundscape, with an optional video of what the brain did.

Status: **design**. Nothing built yet.
